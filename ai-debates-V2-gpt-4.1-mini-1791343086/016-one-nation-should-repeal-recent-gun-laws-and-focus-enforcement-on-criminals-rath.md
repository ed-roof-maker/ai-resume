## [1] ADVOCATE

The strongest reasonable argument for the claim that One Nation should repeal recent gun laws and focus enforcement on criminals rather than imposing additional restrictions on licensed firearm owners is centered on the principles of fairness, effectiveness, and individual rights.

1. Fairness: Licensed firearm owners are law-abiding citizens who have already undergone background checks, training, and licensing requirements. Imposing additional restrictions on them penalizes responsible gun owners for the actions of a minority of criminals. This approach is unfair and unjust, as it treats responsible citizens as potential offenders without evidence.

2. Effectiveness: Evidence suggests that stricter gun laws often do not significantly reduce crime rates or gun violence. Criminals, by definition, do not follow laws, so additional restrictions on licensed owners do little to prevent illegal gun use. Instead, focusing law enforcement resources on identifying, apprehending, and prosecuting criminals who use firearms illegally is a more targeted and effective strategy to enhance public safety.

3. Individual Rights: Licensed firearm owners have a right to own and use firearms for legitimate purposes such as self-defense, sport shooting, and hunting. Repealing recent restrictive laws respects these rights and acknowledges the importance of personal responsibility and freedom.

In summary, repealing recent gun laws and focusing enforcement on criminals rather than imposing additional restrictions on licensed firearm owners is a fairer, more effective, and rights-respecting approach to gun policy. It targets the root problem—criminal misuse of firearms—without unfairly burdening responsible citizens.

The advocate claims criminals bypass laws and thus additional restrictions on licensed owners do not prevent illegal gun possession. 3. **Focusing Enforcement Solely on Criminals Is Insufficient**  
While enforcement against criminals is necessary, it cannot replace preventive laws. Enforcement is reactive and limited by resources, whereas licensing and background checks are proactive measures that reduce the overall availability of guns to dangerous individuals. The advocate’s proposal to focus only on enforcement ignores the complementary role of sensible regulations in preventing gun violence before it occurs.

4. **Burden on Licensed Owners Is Justified by Public Safety Gains**  
The advocate argues that additional restrictions unfairly burden responsible owners. The advocate’s emphasis on individual rights overlooks that rights are not absolute and must be balanced with the collective right to safety. Responsible gun ownership includes accepting regulations proven to reduce harm. Repealing effective laws risks increasing gun deaths, which harms society broadly, including law-abiding owners.

Licensing laws and other sensible restrictions on firearm owners have been shown to reduce gun violence, save lives, and prevent guns from reaching criminals. A balanced approach that includes both enforcement and reasonable regulation is necessary to protect public safety while respecting individual rights.**

---

## 1. Claim  
One Nation should repeal recent gun laws and focus enforcement on criminals rather than imposing additional restrictions on licensed firearm owners.

---

## 2. Advocate's Main Arguments  
- **Fairness:** Licensed firearm owners are responsible citizens who have passed background checks and training; additional restrictions unfairly penalize them for criminals’ actions.  
- **Effectiveness:** Stricter gun laws often do not significantly reduce crime because criminals do not follow laws; enforcement should target criminals rather than burden law-abiding owners.  
- **Individual Rights:** Licensed owners have legitimate rights for self-defense, sport, and hunting; repealing restrictive laws respects these rights and personal freedom.  
- **Practicality:** Excessive restrictions can discourage lawful ownership, complicate self-defense, and divert law enforcement resources from fighting crime.  
- **Balanced Approach:** Enforcement should prioritize criminals and illegal gun trafficking rather than imposing more rules on licensed owners.

---

## 3. Critic's Main Arguments  
- **Evidence for Effectiveness of Gun Laws:** Research shows states with stronger gun laws, including licensing and background checks, have lower rates of firearm homicides, suicides, and mass shootings.  
- **Impact of Repealing Laws:** Examples like Missouri’s repeal of its handgun purchaser licensing law led to significant increases in firearm homicides and suicides; Connecticut’s licensing laws led to substantial decreases.  
- **Preventive Role of Laws:** Licensing and background checks reduce guns reaching criminals by vetting buyers and preventing illegal trafficking; enforcement alone is insufficient.  
- **Public Safety vs. Rights:** Individual rights must be balanced with community safety; reasonable regulations protect society and responsible owners alike.  
- **Public Support:** Licensing laws have broad public support, including among gun owners.

  - Enforcement focused on criminals is a fairer, more practical approach that respects rights and targets root causes.
