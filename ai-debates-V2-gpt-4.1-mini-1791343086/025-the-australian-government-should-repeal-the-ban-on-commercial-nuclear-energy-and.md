## [1] ADVOCATE

The Australian government should repeal the ban on commercial nuclear energy and integrate nuclear power as a central component of the national energy mix for several compelling reasons:

1. **Reliable and Low-Carbon Energy Source**: Nuclear power provides a stable and continuous supply of electricity, unlike intermittent renewable sources such as solar and wind. This reliability is crucial for meeting Australia's growing energy demands and ensuring grid stability. Importantly, nuclear energy produces virtually no greenhouse gas emissions during operation, making it a powerful tool in combating climate change and meeting Australia's emissions reduction targets.

2. **Energy Security and Independence**: By developing domestic nuclear energy capabilities, Australia can reduce its dependence on fossil fuels and imported energy technologies. This enhances national energy security by diversifying the energy mix and reducing vulnerability to global fuel price fluctuations and supply disruptions.

3. **Economic Benefits and Job Creation**: The nuclear industry can stimulate economic growth through high-skilled job creation in construction, operation, and maintenance of nuclear facilities. It also promotes technological innovation and infrastructure development, positioning Australia as a leader in advanced energy technologies.

4. **Utilization of Australia's Uranium Resources**: Australia possesses some of the world's largest uranium reserves. Leveraging these resources for domestic nuclear power generation adds value to the resource beyond export and supports sustainable resource management.

5. **Complement to Renewables**: Nuclear power can complement renewable energy by providing baseload power, enabling a more balanced and resilient energy system. This integration supports a transition to a low-carbon economy without compromising energy reliability.

In summary, repealing the ban on commercial nuclear energy aligns with Australia's environmental goals, energy security needs, and economic interests. It offers a pragmatic and forward-looking solution to the challenges of energy transition.
