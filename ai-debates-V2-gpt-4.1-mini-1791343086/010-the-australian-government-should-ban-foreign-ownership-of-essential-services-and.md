2. **Foreign Investment Does Not Guarantee Housing Accessibility or Affordability for Australians**  
While foreign capital can fund new developments, it does not ensure these homes are accessible or affordable to Australian residents. Essential services like water, energy, and telecommunications are critical to national security and public welfare. 4. **Government Policy Reflects a Balanced but Firm Stance**

5. **Addressing Housing Shortages Requires Multiple Measures, Including Demand Management**  
While increasing housing supply is critical, managing demand is equally important. Banning foreign ownership of residential property is a demand-side measure that complements supply-side reforms. Ignoring demand pressures from foreign ownership risks undermining efforts to improve affordability.

The government’s extension of foreign purchase bans and vacancy fees confirms the policy’s importance. Moreover, protecting essential services from foreign control is vital for national security and public welfare.
