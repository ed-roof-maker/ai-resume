### Strongest Evidence Against the Claim

No meaningful evidence directly contradicts the narrow claim that **certain** antipsychotics **can** cause twitching.

The strongest challenges concern the scope of the supporting arguments:

- The reported **5–10%** rate combines twitches, tremors, and coordination problems; it does not establish the prevalence of twitching alone.
- Evidence about dystonia, parkinsonism, or tremor cannot automatically establish myoclonus.
- Case reports provide limited information about frequency and may leave alternative causes or contributing medications unresolved.

These limitations weaken broader assertions about prevalence and attribution, rather than demonstrating that the adverse effect does not exist.
