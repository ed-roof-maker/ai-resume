These underlying factors may be the real drivers of poor health outcomes rather than lifestyle choices alone. Addressing these broader social determinants might be more impactful than focusing narrowly on exercise, diet, or sleep.

5. **Need for More Rigorous Research**: Reviews and meta-analyses call for more high-quality randomized controlled trials to clarify the role of lifestyle factors in schizophrenia management. Current evidence is promising but preliminary and insufficient to claim lifestyle changes as a significant management strategy.

In summary, while lifestyle factors can support general health, their direct influence on managing schizophrenia symptoms is limited by practical challenges, weak causal evidence, and modest impact on core symptoms. The claim overstates the role of lifestyle modifications relative to established medical and psychosocial treatments.
