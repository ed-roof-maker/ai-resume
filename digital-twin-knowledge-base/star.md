# STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result)

## Technical problem-solving
### Tell me about a time you solved a particularly difficult technical problem. What was the problem, how did you investigate it, and what was the outcome?


### Describe a situation where you had to debug an application that was failing unexpectedly.

### Tell me about a time you improved the performance or reliability of a system.

## Teamwork and communication
### Describe a time you disagreed with another developer or technical colleague. How did you resolve it?

### Tell me about a time you had to explain a complex technical issue to a non-technical stakeholder.

### Give an example of when you collaborated with people from different departments to deliver a technical solution.


## Project management and delivery
### Tell me about a project where you had to work under a tight deadline.

### Describe a time when project requirements changed halfway through development.

### Give an example of when you had to prioritise several competing technical tasks.

## Learning and adaptability
### Tell me about a time you had to learn a new programming language, framework or technology quickly.

### Describe a situation where your original technical approach failed. What did you learn?

### Tell me about a time you independently developed a technical skill and applied it to a real project.


## Initiative and responsibility
### Describe a time you identified a technical problem before anyone else noticed it.

### Tell me about a time you made a mistake in a project. How did you handle it?

### Give an example of a technical improvement you initiated without being asked.
